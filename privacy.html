Privacy Policy

IVE POV is a personal, single-user tool. It is used only by its owner to upload videos the owner filmed to the owner's own YouTube channel.

  • Data accessed: the owner's YouTube channel (to confirm the channel and upload videos) and the owner's own Google Drive folder (read-only, to read source videos).
  • Data use: only to upload the owner's videos. No data is sold, shared, or used for advertising.
  • Other users: the tool does not collect any data from anyone other than the owner.
  • Storage: access tokens are stored privately by the owner and can be revoked at any time at Google Account permissions.
